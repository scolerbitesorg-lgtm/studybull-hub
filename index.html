Good to know

Thoda aur jaan lo.

StudyBull par abhi kya available hai?

Existing StudyBull mock test portal ka direct link, yahin 5-question sample quiz aur ek original maths revision sheet available hain. Course library aur APK downloads ki jagah taiyaar hai; content add hone par yahin dikhega.

Kya account banana zaroori hai?

Is home page aur quick quiz ke liye nahi. My library sirf is browser mein save hoti hai, account ya cloud sync nahi hai. External projects ki apni login requirements ho sakti hain.

Courses aur APKs kab aayenge?

Abhi koi launch date fix nahi hai. Asli course details aur APK files add hone tak inhe coming soon dikhaya jayega. Koi payment ya fake download button nahi hai.
